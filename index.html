About

I chase the boring paperwork nobody else wants.

I've spent the last six years writing about the systems that run quietly in the background of ordinary life — banking, housing, labor — and the people who have to live inside them. Most of my reporting starts with a public records request and ends up being about something much smaller and more human than the data suggested.

Before freelancing, I covered city government for a regional paper that no longer exists, which taught me most of what I know about reading a budget line and sitting through a three-hour hearing without losing the thread.
